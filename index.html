Submission sequence

What a proposal needs

One form, three answers. Most teams put it together in an afternoon — which is exactly why the last week is still worth entering.

  1. Name the problem

    Pick a real health problem in a real place — a community, a workplace, a city, an institution. Specific beats sweeping.

  2. Show the Taiwanese engine

    State which Taiwanese products, technologies, services or business models your solution runs on, and why they are the right fit.

  3. Quantify the impact

    Who benefits, how many, and how you would know it worked. Judges score feasibility as hard as ambition.

Selection ladder

Every stage pays something back
